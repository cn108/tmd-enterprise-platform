# Project management manual

## 1. Project setup

Create the project with a unique code, name, customer, manager, department, cost centre and dates. New projects start as **draft**. Activate only when the customer contract and budget are ready.

## 2. Budgets and contracts

- Record the customer contract and its value.
- Create a budget, then submit it for approval. Only the latest approved budget is used for available-budget calculations.
- Budget revisions require a reason and a new version; do not overwrite history.
- Attach signed contracts and approvals as private documents.

## 3. Milestones

Record milestone name, due date and completion date. A missed due date is shown as overdue. The milestone is completed only when the completion date is recorded.

## 4. Procurement and stock

Project purchases use the normal purchase-request and purchase-order flow. Orders carry the project. Stock issued to the project reduces inventory and adds actual project cost. A purchase order is a commitment, not an incurred cost.

## 5. Cost and profitability

Project actual cost combines approved supplier invoices, project stock issues and approved direct costs. Open commitments remain separate. Estimated margin is:

`contract value − actual cost − open commitments`

The result is an **estimate**, not recognised revenue or a posted profit. Revenue recognition is disabled pending approved accounting policy.

## 6. Services and equipment

Work orders can be linked to a project. Equipment is reserved, dispatched, returned and inspected through the Leasing workspace. Link service reports and photographs to the work order.

## 7. Reports

**Reports → Projects** shows budget versus actual, commitments, estimated margin and milestones. Use the period filter. Do not present estimated margin to management as audited profit.

## 8. Close-out

1. Complete all milestones and work orders.
2. Confirm all receipts, invoices and stock issues are recorded.
3. Reconcile project cost to the ledger and approved documents.
4. Resolve open commitments.
5. Record the actual completion date and close the project.
