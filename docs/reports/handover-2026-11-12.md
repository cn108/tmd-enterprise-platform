### 2.3 Stage 7B invoice posting implementation

Implemented a controlled posting service:

- `backend/apps/invoicing/posting.py`
- `backend/apps/invoicing/exceptions.py`
- public `post_system_journal` wrapper in `backend/apps/accounting/services.py`

Posting behaviour:

- Only approved invoices can post.
- `InvoiceSettings.can_issue` must be true.
- Test invoices are refused.
- A duplicate post is refused through the invoice's existing journal link.
- The invoice date must fall inside an open accounting period.
- Accounts are resolved through approved, effective account-role mappings.
- Supplier invoice:
  - debits product lines to `INVENTORY`
  - debits project invoices to `PROJECT_COST`
  - debits service/other lines to `COST_OF_SALES`
  - debits recoverable VAT to `INPUT_TAX`
  - adds non-recoverable VAT to the cost line
  - credits `AP_CONTROL`
- Customer invoice:
  - debits `AR_CONTROL`
  - credits `REVENUE`
  - credits `OUTPUT_TAX`
- Creates a posted, balanced `JournalEntry`.
- Links the journal back to the invoice.
- Writes `supplier_invoice.posted` / `customer_invoice.posted` audit events.

UI additions:

- **Post to ledger** action for approved invoices.
- **Ledger journal** field in invoice details.
- Permissions granted to System Administrator, Accountant and Finance Controller.
