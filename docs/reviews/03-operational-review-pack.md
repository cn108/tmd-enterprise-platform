# Operational Review Pack

**Purpose:** replace the proposed workflows in `04-workflows.md` with what actually happens, evidenced by documents and signed off by the department that does the work.
**Pack version:** 1.0 — 11 November 2026
**Sessions:** procurement · warehouse · projects/operations · finance operations

**Method for every session:**

1. Walk one **real, recent transaction** end to end out loud, in sequence, naming who touched it at each step.
2. Collect the **blank forms** used (or redacted copies).
3. Mark every step where something is **approved, checked or counted**, and by whom.
4. Test the **scenario cards** at the end of each section — these are the cases a system usually gets wrong.
5. Sign the route; unsigned routes stay PROPOSED.

A useful discipline: if the described process and the actual form disagree, the form is usually right — the description is what people wish happened.
